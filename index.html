Erasure

New

Erasure builds a list of the online accounts you have and tells you how hard each one is to delete. Import a password export, browser history, email or bookmarks, and it finds the sites you signed up for.

For accounts that are hard to close, it writes the deletion request for you.

  • ReadsPasswords CSV, browser history, email export, bookmarks
  • RatesEasy, medium, hard or impossible to delete
  • PrivacyYour files stay in the browser tab and are never uploaded
